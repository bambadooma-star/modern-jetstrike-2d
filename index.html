ADVANCED MANEUVERS

THE ART OF SOLDAT MOVEMENT

Master momentum conservation, 360° somersaults, rocket jumps, and subterranean vertical navigation.

TECH 01

Jet Boost Flight & Hover

Your boots pack high-thrust propellant that recharges automatically when on the ground. Tap or hold SHIFT or RMB to hover, counteract downward gravity, or sprint horizontally across gaps.

TECH 02

360° Somersault Backflip

Jump backwards while aiming in reverse to trigger acrobatic somersaults. Your soldier smoothly rotates 360° while maintaining aim on the target.

TECH 03

Floor Drop-Through

Press S + W or ↓ + ↑ while standing on one-way steel grates to immediately drop down and surprise enemies lurking below.

TECH 04

Rocket Jumping

Aim an RPG warhead at the ground beneath you and jump just as it detonates. The blast wave launches you high into the sky at supersonic velocity.
