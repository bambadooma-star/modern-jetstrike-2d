ENGINE ARCHITECTURE

Retro Soldat-Style Physics

Engineered for momentum conservation, 360° acrobatic aerial somersaults, swept continuous collision, and tactical terrain elasticity.

Jet Boots 360° Flight

Burn thruster propellant with Right-Click or Shift to glide across chasms, out-maneuver heavy artillery, and execute Soldat-style somersaults mid-air.

Living Dynamic Arenas

Combat across 6 diverse biomes including subterranean fortresses, tropical rainforests, sandstone canyons, arctic bastions, and industrial rainy harbors.

Rigid Physical Crates

Zero static lines. Health and grenade crates are rigid physics bodies that tumble, slide, and react dynamically to bullet impact and explosion shockwaves.

Charged Grenades & Bounces

Hold [E] to charge throw power from close-range toss to maximum distance artillery strikes. Elastic grenade physics ricochet off terrain with timed fuses.

Continuous Swept Ballistics

Liang-Barsky swept continuous collision prevents bullet tunnelling through walls. Features authentic velocity, gravity arcs, and steel ricochets.

Tactical Squad Bot AI

Autonomous bots that capture flags, escort carriers, navigate navmesh waypoints, vault obstacles, search for medkits, and engage in tactical AI vs AI wars.
